About

Jonny Gaynor

Mechanical Engineer · Medical Device Designer · Miami Beach, FL

I'm a mechanical engineer pursuing my MS at Columbia University, specializing in medical device design, prototyping, and production. My work sits at the intersection of engineering precision and human impact — building devices that improve lives.

Growing up in Miami Beach, I developed a drive to create things that matter. From robotic arms and catheter prototypes at Penn State to playing college baseball with my brother at Grinnell, every experience has sharpened both my technical skill and my perspective on what engineering is really for.

At Columbia, I'm focused on translating that foundation into medical devices that reach patients who need them.
